ABOUT MAYON PADAI

Where Archaeology Meets
Science and Technology

Mayon Padai is the Archaeometallurgy arm of the Department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering at National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli, dedicated to the interdisciplinary study of ancient metallurgical technologies, archaeological materials, technological heritage, and digital reconstruction.

The collective brings together expertise from metallurgy, materials science, archaeology, scientific characterization, artificial intelligence, computational modelling, and 3D reconstruction.

Our research begins with archaeological artefacts and fragments and seeks to uncover the technological knowledge preserved within them.

OUR VISION

To Reveal the Technological
Heritage of Humanity

To advance the understanding of ancient technologies through archaeology, materials science and AI-based 3D reconstruction, preserving scientific knowledge for future research.

OUR MISSION

To investigate archaeological artefacts through composition, microstructure, morphology and manufacturing evidence. We combine interdisciplinary analysis and computational methods to interpret production technologies and reconstruct plausible forms of incomplete artefacts.

PROCESS ANALYSIS

Archaeometallurgy

Ferrous and non-ferrous systems: evaluating evidence of smelting, refining, casting, forging and alloying.

MATERIALS CHARACTERISATION

Composition & structure

Composition, phases, microstructure and porosity: investigating relationships between material features and processing.

COMPUTATIONAL METHODS

AI-Based 3D Reconstruction

Computer vision, geometric analysis and AI-assisted modelling to analyse features and estimate plausible missing geometry.

Reconstruction protocol: distinguish measured geometry, scientific interpretation and hypothesised features; validate against available evidence.
